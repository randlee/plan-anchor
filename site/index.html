plan-anchor

Keeps plans, and the code built from them, anchored to what you actually said while planning.

Facts you state are captured verbatim as numbered anchors, turned into typed questions for the Jev decision model, and enforced mechanically at two gates: once against the plan, once against the delivered work. When something drifts, the report quotes your own words back.
